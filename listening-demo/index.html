Setup takes one minute:

  1. Unzip the folder. It contains index.html and the tracks Song1.mp3 … Song27.mp3. Keep them all in the same folder.
  2. Open index.html — double-click to launch it in a browser (Chrome or Safari).
  3. Pick the exam (Part One / Part Two) at the top, then click "Select your music folder" and choose that exam's folder. It turns green once the tracks load.
  4. Pick settings and hit Begin session.
